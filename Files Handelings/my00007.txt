A Quiet Hope:-
When morning paints the sky in gold,
The sleepy world begins to wake,
The dreams we carried through the night
Become the paths we choose to take.
A gentle breeze moves through the trees,
And whispers, “Do not fear the day.”
Though clouds may gather overhead,
The sun will find its quiet way.
We stumble, learn, and rise again,
With courage growing through the pain,
For every storm that tests our hearts
Will leave behind a softer rain.
So hold your hope and let it grow,
For brighter days are yet to show.
